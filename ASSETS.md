# Rundfunk asset provenance and licensing

This document records bundled visual assets, derived source material, licenses, and redistribution notes.

Wallpaper videos are sourced from Pixabay and other media platforms. This document records bundled visual assets, source mappings, and technical/derivation notes for the current release.

## Bundled wallpapers (static, selectable) and native intro reveals

Rundfunk ships **sixteen** static 16:9 wallpaper images in `backgrounds/` as selectable/active wallpapers and **sixteen** matching one-time intro reveal videos in `backgrounds/intros/` (same filename stems) for Omarchy 4.5's native intro mechanism. Each intro is designed to play once on eligible theme-switch events and resolve into its exactly matching static wallpaper composition; no continuous/looping desktop video playback is used. Three previously included pairs were removed after owner testing and are **not included** in the release: `2-sunset-palm-road`, `13-velvet-horizon`, `14-aurora-veil` (see removed-pairs section below). Remaining wallpapers were not renumbered.
